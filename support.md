# AntiStress — Support

Need help with AntiStress? Write to **mukashi.dev@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**The sound doesn't play.** Check that Sound is on (tap the grid button, then Settings) and that your iPhone's silent switch is off. (While a sleep timer runs, sound plays even with the silent switch on.)

**How do I use the sleep timer?** Tap the grid button, open Settings, then choose a length under Sleep timer. The world's sound keeps playing with the screen locked and fades out at the end.

**The daily reminder doesn't arrive.** Tap the grid button, open Settings and turn on Daily reminder, and allow notifications for AntiStress in iOS Settings. Reminders are scheduled two weeks ahead and refreshed each time you open the app.

**How do I save Breathe sessions to Apple Health?** Tap the grid button, open Settings, turn on "Save Breathe to Health" and allow AntiStress to write Mindful Minutes. Only finished sessions are saved.

**How do I add the widget?** Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose AntiStress. Edit the widget to pick which world it shows.

**How do I turn off notifications?** Go to iOS Settings → Notifications → AntiStress. The daily reminder can also be switched off in the app: tap the grid button, then Settings.

**How do I learn what to do in a world?** Open the world, tap its settings button, then How to play.

**What is AntiStress Pro?** Bubbles is free for everyone, and every other world can be played once, for as long as you like. AntiStress Pro opens every world, and each new one as it arrives: by the month, by the year (starting with a 3-day free trial) or once for life. If you installed AntiStress before Pro arrived, Pond, Bubbles and Window stay yours for good.

**Can my family use Pro?** Yes, Lifetime can be shared with up to five family members through Apple's Family Sharing. Subscriptions are for the person who buys them.

**How do I get Pro back on a new device?** Tap the grid button, open Settings, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. The Pro offer has Restore too.

**How do I cancel or change my subscription?** In the app, tap the grid button, open Settings, then Manage Subscription; or go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

**How do I ask for a refund?** Apple handles refunds: go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Does AntiStress collect my data?** No account, no ads, no tracking. The app sends usage statistics and crash reports that don't identify you, so we can see which worlds people enjoy and fix problems. See the [Privacy Policy](./).
