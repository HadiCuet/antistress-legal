AntiStress — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

AntiStress is a collection of calm, touchable worlds for iPhone and iPad. It is built to be private: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. To learn which worlds people enjoy and to fix problems, the app sends usage statistics and crash reports that don't identify you. This policy explains the little data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

AntiStress is developed and operated by Abdullah Al Hadi ("we", "us"). For the EU / UK / EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Abdullah Al Hadi is the data controller for personal data processed through the app. For any privacy question, write to mukashi.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data the app handles

Stays only on your device

The app keeps a few things on your device so it works the way you left it. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple includes them in your encrypted backup; that is between you and Apple. This stored data is never sent to us. The usage statistics described below do note which options and lengths you pick, but never your check-ins, your photo, your sounds or your reminder time.

  • Settings: the world you used last, Sound and Haptics, each world's options (in Sleep: your mixes, each place's sound levels, when the sound fades and the alarm), the reminder time and the sleep timer length.
  • Breathe check-ins: if you rate how tense you feel before and after a Breathe session, those ratings.
  • Your calm this week: how long you spent in the worlds and how many Breathe sessions you finished, shown in Settings.
  • Your photo: if you choose one of your photos for Bubbles or Leaves, one smaller copy of it for that world, so the world can show it next time. Picking another photo replaces it.
  • Your sounds: if you add your own audio from Files, a copy of each file, made into a seamless loop, and its name. Removing a sound deletes its copy.
  • Sleep's nights: the place Sleep opens on, tonight's or last night's place and how long it played, and the stars your nights have earned, for "Last night" and the Lock Screen widget. This is kept in your device's keychain, where the widget can read it.
  • Pictures of the worlds: small pictures of each world for the Worlds gallery, kept in the app's cache.
  • First launch: whether you have seen the welcome pages, and the world suggested by your answers to "What brings you here?" until you open a world. The answers themselves are not kept.
  • App bookkeeping: which guides you have seen.
  • AntiStress Pro: which worlds have had their free visit, which Sleep places and mixes have had their free night, and whether this copy of the app was first installed before AntiStress Pro existed (those installs keep Pond, Bubbles and Window free). This is kept in your device's keychain, so deleting and reinstalling the app doesn't reset it. The app also remembers whether it found Pro the last time it checked, so your worlds open straight away.

Roll, Leaves, Bubble Wrap and Window read your device's motion, for example to roll the ball or move the leaves as you tilt. The motion is used as it happens and is never stored or sent.

Purchases (Apple)

AntiStress Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple takes the payment and handles your Apple Account; we never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The app asks Apple whether this Apple Account has Pro, and once, which version of the app was first downloaded, so people who installed it before Pro keep their free worlds. If you share Lifetime through Family Sharing, Apple decides who is in your family; the app only learns that Pro is available. With Ask to Buy, the purchase waits for the family organizer's approval in Apple's own flow.

When you buy Pro, the usage statistics below note which plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime), whether it began with the free trial, and where the offer was shown; never the price, and never who you are.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Firebase Analytics, a Google service, tells us how the app is used, counted across everyone, so we can decide what to improve and which worlds to build next. It runs without the advertising identifier and with Google's advertising features turned off. It collects:

  • A per-install identifier: a random Firebase app-instance ID that belongs to this installation of AntiStress only and changes if you reinstall the app. The app never reads your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and Analytics is set not to collect its identifier for vendors (IDFV).
  • Basic device information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, and your approximate location (country, region and city), which Google works out from your IP address.
  • How the app is used: which worlds and screens you open and for how long; where a world was opened from (the gallery, the widget, Siri or a notification); when a Breathe session starts and finishes, and its length; when you take or save a picture with "Save this moment"; when the sleep timer starts, and its length; when music or a podcast from another app first plays under a world; when you add one of your own sounds, and its length in minutes (never the file or its name).
  • Your first launch: which welcome pages you see, whether you finish or skip them, and the answers you tick on "What brings you here?" (such as better sleep or anxious moments); your answer to the reminder offered on first launch and to the notification permission request.
  • Your choices: the options you pick, such as Sound, Haptics, the daily reminder, News, and each world's scene, style, ground or photo setting (never the photo itself).
  • AntiStress Pro: when a world's free visit or a Sleep place's or mix's free night starts; when the AntiStress Pro offer opens and closes, and where it was opened from (such as the gallery or a Sleep place); the plan bought (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and whether it began with the free trial; whether Restore found a purchase; and when the app asks for a rating.
  • We never send your Breathe check-in ratings, your calm this week, the word you type to color the bubbles, your photos or pictures, your own sounds, your nights in Sleep, your reminder time, anything about Apple Health, or anything that identifies you. We never set a user ID.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, sends a report when the app crashes, or when part of it fails quietly, such as a world that can't draw or a sound that can't start. A report holds the technical details of the failure (a stack trace), the state of the device at that moment (such as model, iOS version and free memory), the world that was on screen, the last few usage events before it, and a per-install Crashlytics identifier. It holds none of your content.

Notifications from us (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, delivers the app's occasional notifications, such as a note that a new world has arrived.

  • We store no device tokens, not on your device and not on a server. We have no user database.
  • Every message is addressed to a topic, not to a person. Your device joins two: one for everyone, and one for the language the app is shown in. That membership tells Google's delivery service the app's language, and nothing else.
  • Because we send to topics, we cannot target an individual person. Everyone on a topic gets the same message.
  • To deliver a notification at all, Google issues a registration token for this installation of the app. It is per-install and resets when the app is reinstalled.
  • Notifications are optional; see Section 3.

The daily reminder you can turn on is different: it is scheduled on your device and never involves a server.

Emails to us

If you write to us, from the app or otherwise, we receive your email address and your message. When you write from the app's Settings, the draft also includes a short device report you can read and edit before sending: the app version, iOS version, device model, language and region setting, and the world you were in. We use it only to answer you.

3. Permissions the app may ask for

Each one is optional:

  • Notifications: for the daily reminder and the occasional notification from us. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → AntiStress.
  • Apple Health (write only): if you allow it, finished Breathe sessions are saved to Health as Mindful Minutes. The app asks after your first finished Breathe session. AntiStress never reads anything from Health. Health data is handled by Apple under Apple's terms.
  • Photos (add only): "Save this moment" adds the picture you took to your photo library. The app can't see your existing photos.
  • Alarms (iOS 26 and later): if you choose to wake at dawn in Sleep, iOS asks to let AntiStress set the alarm. It is set on your device and nothing is sent.
  • A photo you choose: for "Your photo", iOS's photo picker hands the app only the photo you select; the app has no access to the rest of your library. It keeps the smaller copy described in Section 2, and the photo is never sent anywhere.
  • A sound you choose: for "Your sounds", iOS's Files picker hands the app only the file you select. It keeps the copy described in Section 2, and the sound is never sent anywhere.

4. How we use this data

  • On-device data powers the app's features: picking up where you left off and applying your settings. It is used for nothing else.
  • Firebase Analytics tells us, across everyone, which worlds and features get used, so we can decide what to improve and what to build next. We look at totals and trends, not at individuals.
  • Firebase Crashlytics tells us when and why the app fails, so we can fix it.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging lets us tell people about new worlds and features, in their own language.
  • Emails are used only to reply to you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not share data with advertisers, and we do not build profiles across other apps.

5. Children

AntiStress is suitable for everyone, including children. It has no accounts, no ads and no chat, and asks for no personal information. The only data that leaves the device is the usage statistics, crash reports and notification topic membership described above, none of which identifies anyone. Purchases go through Apple, so a child's purchase can wait for a parent's approval with Ask to Buy.

6. Third parties

Apart from Apple, the only services the app communicates with are Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging, operated by Google LLC. Google processes this data as our service provider, under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. Google's own practices are covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Notifications reach your device through Apple Push Notification service, operated by Apple. Purchases, Family Sharing, Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, Control Center, alarms, AirPlay and Apple Health are Apple features, covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The AntiStress Pro offer asks Apple's App Store for the app's public rating at most once a day; the request carries no personal data. Rate in the app's Settings shows Apple's own rating prompt.

Google primarily processes this data on servers in the United States. For users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, transfers rely on Google's Data Processing and Security Terms, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

No advertising or tracking SDK is included in the app, and the app never reads the advertising identifier.

7. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete the app, which removes everything it stored, including your photo and your sounds, except two small records in the keychain: AntiStress Pro's (used free visits and free nights, and whether the install dates from before Pro) and Sleep's nights for the Lock Screen widget. iOS keeps those after the app is deleted; erasing the device removes them.
  • Usage statistics: Google keeps the individual events for up to 14 months, then deletes them.
  • Crash reports: Google keeps them for 90 days.
  • Notification topic membership: for as long as the app is installed with notifications allowed; Google removes inactive registrations over time.
  • Emails: as long as needed to help you; ask and we will delete them.

8. Your rights and choices

You can turn off notifications, Health saving and Sound at any time, in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings, set Your photo to Off and remove your sounds. Usage statistics and crash reports are tied only to random per-install identifiers, never to your name or an account, so we can't pick out one person's data to export or delete; deleting the app removes everything on your device and retires those identifiers for good. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing; write to us and we will help.

9. Security

The app keeps its data in the iOS app sandbox, and its two small records in the iOS keychain. Communication with Firebase and the App Store is encrypted in transit.
